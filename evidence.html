Tier rules

  • A: Strong ICP + relevant senior role + strong event evidence. Core segment (metal, food, distribution, logistics) with a P2P, O2C or supply chain leader at Head level or above, named as a speaker. Manufacturing qualifies only at the top: VP or C-level, DACH-based, named by the organiser.
  • B: Good company or role fit, but a weaker buying signal or weaker evidence: company-level or undated evidence, adjacent or junior role, manufacturing below the A bar, or retail.
  • C: Weak ICP fit or low relevance: outside target segments, or no buying-relevant function in the published title.
